AR Navigator was a wayfinding prototype: hold up your phone, see the route drawn
onto the world in front of you instead of on an abstract map.

> The original demo was hosted on a `.ml` domain. Freenom stopped issuing and
> resolving those, so the link that used to live here is dead and I've removed
> it rather than leave it rotting.

The lesson I actually took away had nothing to do with AR. It was that a
free domain from a registrar with no business model is a link you're going to
lose. Four years later the code is fine and the URL is gone — which is part of
why this site now sits on infrastructure I control.
